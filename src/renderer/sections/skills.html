Teach an AI assistant to drive Glyphh. Export a Glyphh Skill into Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT — it learns to open apps and layouts, run your installed apps' tools, and orchestrate tasks over the MCP relay.

What is a Glyphh Skill?

A small instruction bundle you drop into your AI tool's skills or custom instructions. Once it's installed and connected to your Glyphh endpoint, the assistant can control your workspace on your behalf — no copy-paste, no manual steps.
