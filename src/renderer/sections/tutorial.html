One AI workspace. One context, every app, every LLM, everywhere.
Add your apps
Bring the web and local apps you already use into one workspace. Open the Apps tab, paste a URL or point at a local build, and it opens as a panel — press ⌘⇧Space to jump to any app.
Connect your systems
Connect the accounts you already have — Slack, GitHub, Notion, Google and more. Do it once from Connections; every app and agent you build can use them, and they never see your tokens.
Connect your AI
Point Claude — or any MCP client — at Glyphh from Settings › MCP. Your AI can then open your apps, run your skills, and use your connected accounts, all on your machine.
Build & host locally
Build apps with any agent and host them right here — vector search, a knowledge graph, cron, and a shareable public URL, all running locally under your control.
