Gateway credits per period. Resets on calendar boundaries — daily at midnight UTC, weekly on Mondays, monthly on the 1st. Blank = none.

Models

The models your organization supports, the providers they run on, and your own custom endpoints. Roles pick from the supported set — nothing outside it can be used.

Frontier models served through the gateway. Toggle which ones your organization supports. A supported model also needs its provider enabled — Providers tab.

Open-weight models — including the zero-credit on-device set run by llama.cpp. Toggle which ones your organization supports.

Point at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — Together, Fireworks, a self-hosted vLLM, anything that speaks /chat/completions. The key is write-only and pays the vendor directly.

Enable the vendors your org runs on. Enabling a provider makes its catalog models reachable; add a BYOK key to have your own key pay the vendor instead of the proxy. Keys are write-only.
